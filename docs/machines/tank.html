Machine · Swedish

Tank

The Tank is the biggest walking war machine of the Swedish (FNIX-built) robots: a heavily armoured four-class family with two heavy weapon mounts, a concussion or neurotoxin ability and a stomp for anyone who gets close.

It comes in 4 classes with 12 distinct layouts between them; total component health ranges from 9,990 to 25,787.5.
