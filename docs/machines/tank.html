The Tank is the biggest walking war machine of the Swedish (FNIX-built) robots: a heavily armoured four-class family with two heavy weapon mounts, a concussion or neurotoxin ability and a stomp for anyone who gets close.

The game files call it dreadnought. It comes in 4 classes with 12 distinct layouts between them; total component health ranges from 9,990 to 25,787.5.

Detection

Unaware, it notices you at up to 132 m standing, 57.75 m crouched and 24.75 m prone (running: 181.5 m). Once alerted, sight grows to 1,320 m standing. It hears you running at 49.5 m and walking at 33 m. It starts searching at a detection score of 1, attacks at 1.9, and the score drains by 0.25 per second; at night sight is multiplied by 0.75.
