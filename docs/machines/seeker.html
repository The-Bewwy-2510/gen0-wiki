Seeker

The Seeker is a small flying scout. It carries no weapons at all: its job is to spot you, and it has some of the sharpest senses of any Swedish machine.

It comes in 4 classes with 4 distinct layouts between them; total component health ranges from 112.6 to 290.

Heads up

The Prototype Seeker is the toughest of its type, not the weakest: 290 total component health against 112.6 for the Military, 150.4 for the FNIX, 187.4 for the Apocalypse. The reason is its Engine Drive: 120 HP each, against 4.7–7.8 HP in the later classes.

Weak points

  • Cheapest components to break: Communications Array (7.5 HP), Fuel Tank (10 HP), Optics (12.5 HP).
  • Fuel tank: 10 HP, a classic weak spot to aim for.
  • 3 armour plates hold 7% of the total (20 HP); the thinnest are 5 HP. Break plates with ammo that has a high armour multiplier, then switch to ammo with a high component multiplier for the parts underneath (see choosing ammo).

Detection

Unaware, it notices you at up to 112 m standing, 49 m crouched and 21 m prone (running: 154 m). Once alerted, sight grows to 1.12 km standing. It hears you running at 42 m and walking at 28 m. Its vision cone is 90° wide. It starts searching at a detection score of 1, attacks at 1.9, and the score drains by 0.25 per second; at night sight is multiplied by 0.75. Full senses table.
