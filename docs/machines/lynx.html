The Lynx is the small, quick Soviet machine with a spinning saw blade on its front and a rifle, grenade launcher or flamethrower on top.

The game files call it lynx. It comes in 3 classes with 6 distinct layouts between them; total component health ranges from 490.4 to 980.5.

Weak points

  • Cheapest components to break: Engine Drive (25 HP ×2), Fuel Tank (25 HP), Burst Assault Rifle (65 HP).
  • Weapons are components you can shoot off: Burst Assault Rifle 65 HP.
  • Fuel tank: 25 HP, a classic weak spot to aim for.
  • 9 armour plates hold 29% of the total (140.4 HP); the thinnest are 15.6 HP. Break plates with ammo that has a high armour multiplier, then switch to ammo with a high component multiplier for the parts underneath (see choosing ammo).

Detection

Unaware, it notices you at up to 100 m standing, 43.75 m crouched and 18.75 m prone (running: 137.5 m). Once alerted, sight grows to 1,000 m standing. It hears you running at 37.5 m and walking at 25 m. It starts searching at a detection score of 1, attacks at 1.9, and the score drains by 0.25 per second; at night sight is multiplied by 0.75.
