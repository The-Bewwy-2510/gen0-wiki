The Hunter is the fast, agile mid-sized Swedish machine: a gun on its back, blades for jump and melee attacks, and it can be flown in as reinforcements in a rocket pod.

The game files call it hunter. It comes in 4 classes with 9 distinct layouts between them; total component health ranges from 880 to 3,542.4.
