Difficulty & co-op

Generation Zero scales damage in two directions: how much damage you do to machines, and how much damage machines do to you. Both depend on the difficulty you play on and on the number of players in the session. This page lists the multipliers from the game's difficulty settings, explains what they mean in practice and has a calculator.

From the game files
The multipliers are stored with two decimals in the data, so 0.67 and 1.33 are most likely ⅔ and 1⅓, and 0.63 / 0.38 most likely 0.625 / 0.375.

What stands out

  • More players means harder machines on every difficulty. Each extra player lowers everyone's damage and raises the damage machines do. The difficulty settings hold only these two damage multipliers, so this is how the game balances co-op.
  • Medium co-op lowers your damage. Medium is the ×1 baseline only when you play alone. With one friend you already do ×0.83 and take ×1.33; with four players you do ×0.5 (half) and take ×2 (double).
  • Easy with 4 players is exactly Medium solo (×1 / ×1). Easy is the only difficulty where you ever do more than base damage: ×2 solo, falling to ×1 with four players.
  • Hard solo already doubles machine damage (×2) and cuts yours to ×0.75. Hard with 4 players is the extreme: you do ×0.38 and take ×4. Compared with Easy solo, a hit on you is 8 times as strong and your shots are 5.3 times weaker.
  • Swapping one difficulty step is worth about one or two players. Medium with 3 players (×0.67 / ×1.67) is close to Hard solo (×0.75 / ×2).

Team damage

If every player fires at the same machine all the time, the group's combined damage is the number of players times the multiplier. This table shows that total compared with one player on Medium solo (×1), rounded to one decimal because the multipliers themselves are rounded.

The fourth player adds almost nothing to the group's firepower: on Medium three players together do ×2 and four do ×2; on Hard it is ×1.5 against ×1.5. What a bigger group gains is more targets for the machines, more angles and more people to revive you, while each of you takes more damage.

Worked examples

Your .44 Magnus on Hard, solo

The .44 Full Metal Jacket Ammo does 56 per bullet at full quality (×1 for Q5). On Hard solo your damage is ×0.75: 56 × 0.75 = 42 per hit. With a Worn (Q2) copy, which has a quality multiplier of ×0.62, it is 56 × 0.62 × 0.75 = 26.04. Armour and component multipliers of the ammo apply on top.

A Runner's Auto Shotgun against you, Medium with 4 players

It fires 6 pellets of 10: 60 per shot if every pellet hits. Medium with 4 players doubles machine damage (×2): 60 × 2 = 120 per shot, or 20 per pellet. Alone on Medium the same blast does 60.

A Tank's Linear Accelerator Cannon across the difficulties

90 per hit at base. Easy solo: 45. Hard solo: 180. Hard with 4 players: 360.

A level 4 rival on Hard, solo

Rival damage multipliers stack with difficulty. A level 4 rival does ×2 damage; on Hard solo that is ×2 × ×2 = ×4. A 10-damage bullet becomes 40. The rival also has ×3 health while you do ×0.75 damage, so it takes 4 times as many hits as the same machine on Medium solo. See Rivals.

Note
We assume the multipliers multiply each other, as their names in the game files suggest. The game files don't say in which order the game rounds them.
