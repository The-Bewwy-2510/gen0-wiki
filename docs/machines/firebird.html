Firebird

The Firebird is the Soviet flying gunship: an autocannon or howitzer, two bomb bays and four vectored-thrust jets keeping it in the air.

It comes in 3 classes with 6 distinct layouts between them; total component health ranges from 5,950 to 11,900.

From the game files

Total component health: All component and armour hit points added up. The game keeps no separate overall health bar value in a readable file, so use this as a size comparison, not an exact kill threshold.

Heads up

All 4 Firebird classes in the files (Scout, Soldier, Spetsnaz, Rival) have exactly the same senses: a higher class does not see or hear you any farther (unaware, 150 m standing and 189 m hearing you run). See detection & stealth.

Detection

Unaware, it notices you at up to 150 m standing, 100 m crouched and 60 m prone (running: 200 m). Once alerted, sight grows to 2.52 km standing. It hears you running at 189 m and walking at 126 m. Its vision cone is 25° wide. It starts searching at a detection score of 1, attacks at 1.9, and the score drains by 0.25 per second; at night sight is multiplied by 0.75. Full senses table.
