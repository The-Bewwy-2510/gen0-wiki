Detection & stealth

Machines notice you with sight and hearing. Each machine class has its own distances for each stance, a vision cone, and a detection score that decides when it starts searching and when it attacks. This page lists the senses of all 37 machines in the game files and the stealth tips that follow from them.

How detection works

From the game files

machine_detection: how far each machine class can see (sight_m) and hear (hearing_m) you per stance. max = farthest distance at which it starts to notice you; full_speed_within = inside this it notices you at the full rate. Detection is a score that builds while you are seen or heard and drains by drain_per_second; at search_at the machine starts searching, at attack_at it attacks. unaware = normal state, alerted = searching or fighting. field_of_view_deg is the width of the vision cone. night_sight_multiplier applies to sight from 19:00 to 07:00.

  • Max is the farthest distance at which a machine starts to notice you at all. Between max and full speed within the detection score builds slowly; inside the full-speed distance it builds at the full rate.
  • The vision cone works the same way: the first angle is the full cone, the second the part of it where you are noticed at the full rate (both in degrees).
  • The detection score rises while the machine sees or hears you and drains while it does not. It starts searching at search at and attacks at attack at.
  • Unaware is the normal state; alerted is while it searches or fights.

Stealth tips from the numbers

  • Get low. For almost every machine, crouching cuts the distance at which it can see you to 44% of the standing distance, and going prone to 19%. Running makes it 138%. Exceptions: Firebirds (and the Vulture), Tick (class x) (see their tables). Careful with the Tick (class x): it sees you farther prone (50 m, 360° cone) than crouched (35 m), so crouch rather than go prone near it.
  • Don't be seen twice. Once a machine is alerted its sight reaches 10 times as far (for example the Tank (Prototype): 88 m becomes 880 m standing). Exceptions: ×7 for Reaper; ×16.8 for Firebird (Scout), Firebird (Soldier), Firebird (Spetsnaz), Vulture. By stance: the Tick (class x)'s prone sight does not grow at all (50 m alerted) instead of ×10; for Firebirds (and the Vulture) the factor depends on your stance, from ×7.88 prone to ×17.32 running. Break line of sight and let it calm down before you move in the open again.
  • The score drains slowly. Nearly every machine searches at 1, attacks at 1.9 and drains 0.25 per second, so a machine at the attack threshold needs 3.6 s without seeing or hearing you to drop back below the search threshold, and 7.6 s for its detection score to drain to 0.
  • Move at night. From 19:00 to 07:00 sight is multiplied by 0.75 for almost every machine: the Tank (Prototype) that sees you standing at 88 m by day sees you at 66 m.
  • Sound carries. Hearing does not care about the vision cone. The best ears in the files (running, unaware): Firebird (Scout) 189 m, Firebird (Soldier) 189 m, Firebird (Spetsnaz) 189 m, Vulture 189 m. Going prone means no machine hears you at the full rate (its full-speed distance is 0 m).
  • Know the spotters. Longest unaware sight standing: Gnome Tick 320 m, Reaper 240 m, Seeker (Apocalypse) 162.4 m, Firebird (Scout) 150 m, Firebird (Soldier) 150 m. Within every regular Swedish class the Seeker is the one that sees farthest.
  • Stay out of the cone. Nearly all ground machines see in a 90° cone and notice you at the full rate only in its central 45°. Firebirds that have not spotted you look through a narrow 10° to 25° cone, which widens to 60° to 180° once alerted.
