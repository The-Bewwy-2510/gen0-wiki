Machine · Swedish

Tick

Ticks are the smallest machines in the game. They rush you and latch on, and some classes explode instead. Bigger machines with a Tick Storage component release them in battle. This page also covers the special Gnome Tick of the events.

It comes in 5 classes with 11 distinct layouts between them; total component health ranges from 12.5 to 31.25.

Detection

Unaware, it notices you at up to 80 m standing, 35 m crouched and 15 m prone (running: 110 m). Once alerted, sight grows to 800 m standing. It hears you running at 30 m and walking at 20 m. Its vision cone is 90° wide. It starts searching at a detection score of 1, attacks at 1.9, and the score drains by 0.25 per second; at night sight is multiplied by 0.75. Full senses table.
