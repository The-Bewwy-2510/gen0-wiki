Ticks are the smallest machines in the game. They rush you and latch on, and some classes explode instead. Bigger machines with a Tick Storage component release them in battle. This page also covers the two special Tick classes in the files, the Gnome Tick and the unidentified Class X Tick.

The game files call it pest. It comes in 6 classes with 12 distinct layouts between them; total component health ranges from 12.5 to 31.25.
