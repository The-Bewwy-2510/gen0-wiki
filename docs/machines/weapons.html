Reading the numbers

All numbers are taken unchanged from the game files. Damage is in hit points, radius in metres and time in seconds.

Direct hit
Damage of one bullet, pellet or shell that hits you. A shotgun-style weapon fires several per shot; the table multiplies them out.
Blast
Area damage where a projectile or attack explodes, separately against you and against machines, each with its own radius in metres. Explosive shells do both: the direct hit and the blast.
Bomblets
Cluster weapons fire one carrier that splits into several bomblets; each bomblet has its own blast.
Damage over time
Burn, gas or radiation: a fixed amount every tick (e.g. every 1 s) for a set duration, refreshed while you stay in it. We add the ticks up to one exposure.
Contact
Damage from a part that hurts you while you touch it (the Lynx saw): a damage range per hit within a small radius, repeating at a fixed interval while you stay in reach.
One hit
What a single projectile or bomblet does to you if it hits: its direct damage, its blast and its burn added up. This is the column to sort by to find the hardest single hits.
Max per attack
Our upper bound for one attack against you: every projectile, blast, bomblet and burn tick lands. Real hits are usually lower (you are at the edge of a blast, a pellet misses), so use it to compare weapons, not as a promise.
Difficulty
All damage to you is multiplied by the difficulty and co-op multiplier (×0.5 to ×4), see Difficulty & co-op. The numbers here are the base values (×1, Medium solo). Each weapon's On difficulty line shows its max per attack on five settings, from the lowest multiplier to the highest: Easy, Medium and Hard (E, M, H) solo, Hard with 2 and Hard with 4 players (H4 = Hard, 4 players; Medium with 4 players has the same ×2 as H1). Scaled values keep one decimal.
