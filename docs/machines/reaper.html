Reaper

The Reaper is the unique Rival-class Tank, by far the toughest machine in the game files. It carries radiated missiles, an incendiary machine gun, Ticks, neurotoxin, a shield against your weapons and a huge thermobaric blast.

It is a Tank of the Rival class, with a total component health of 160,000.

Weak points

  • Cheapest components to break: Part (1,500 HP ×13), Anti-Weapons Shield Emitter (1,800 HP ×4), Fuel Tank (1,800 HP).
  • Weapons are components you can shoot off: Incendiary Machine Gun 13,000 HP, Missile Pod 13,000 HP, Tick Storage 6,000 HP, Neurotoxin Disperser 4,500 HP ×2, Anti-Weapons Shield Emitter 1,800 HP ×4.
  • Fuel tank: 1,800 HP, a classic weak spot to aim for.
  • The Ventilation has an armour plate of 1,500 HP over a 3,400 HP component.
  • 27 armour plates hold 25% of the total (40,500 HP); the thinnest are 1,500 HP. Break plates with ammo that has a high armour multiplier, then switch to ammo with a high component multiplier for the parts underneath (see choosing ammo).

Detection

Unaware, it notices you at up to 240 m standing, 105 m crouched and 45 m prone (running: 330 m). Once alerted, sight grows to 1.68 km standing. It hears you running at 115 m and walking at 85 m. Its vision cone is 90° wide. It starts searching at a detection score of 1, attacks at 1.9, and the score drains by 0.25 per second; at night sight is multiplied by 0.75. Full senses table.
