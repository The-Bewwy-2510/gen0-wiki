Rivals

Rivals are machines that grow stronger over time. Each one has a level, a generated title such as The Tyrant of the Mountain, and a history of what it has done. They level up by gaining experience (the Gen0 Editor's notes say: by killing players or escaping), and each level multiplies their health, damage and reward. How often new rivals appear depends on the threat level of the region. This page has the level table, the threat table, the two unique rival machines and the complete title list from the game files.

Rival titles1398 texts

Every rival gets a title. The game stores them as a text table keyed by a hash (the save game keeps the hash of the rival's title). There are 1,398 texts in the table: 1,184 place titles, 62 titles aimed at people, 148 stand-alone epithets, and 4 texts that belong to the rival's history rather than its name.

How a title is put together

TheEpithetof / fromPlace

Most titles follow the pattern The <epithet> of <place>: 41 epithets (Tsar, Invader, Plague, Liberator ...) combined with 128 places, from map locations like Hagaboda and Averholm Manor to landscape words like the Rugged Wilds or the Cold. Not every combination exists: each epithet appears with 24 to 48 of the places. 27 titles use from instead of of (The Menace from Sävered). The epithets range from menacing (Tyrant, Plague, Horror) to ironically friendly (Liberator, Benefactor, Helper, Savior), and a few have a Soviet ring (Tsar, Kommisar, Iron Zmey, Kikimora, Revolutionary).

A second, smaller set uses the same pattern with people instead of a place: The Bane of Friends, The Exterminator of All Humankind, The Predator of Meatbags. 32 epithets are combined with 13 words for people (Comrades, Companions, Society, Homo Sapiens, All Humankind, the Survivors, Allies, Humanity, Humans, Friends, Meatbags, Teammates, the Resistance). Their wording suggests they are given to rivals that have killed players, but the files we read don't say when each set is used.

The remaining 148 titles stand alone: The Red Savior, The Iron Tsar, General Frost, Koschei the Deathless. Many are built as The <Red / Iron / Mechanical / Terrible / Revolutionary / Loyal> <Soldier / Comrade / General / Agent / Kommissar>, and many borrow names from Russian folklore and myth (Baba Yaga, Koschei, Perun, the Zmey dragon, the Kikimora, and the heroes Ilya Muromets, Dobrynya Nikitich, Alyosha Popovich, Svyatogor and Mikula Selyaninovich), which gives them a Soviet theme.

Four texts in the table are not titles. Two are the rival history entries the game links to by hash (creation and level-up); of the other two, one looks like a history entry and one is a placeholder:

  • Became [TITLE] (history entry when the rival is created (its title fills in [TITLE]); hash 47971389)
  • Defeated a player (probably a history entry; not referenced in the game's history table; hash 1418695187)
  • Evolved to level [LEVEL] (history entry for each level gained ([LEVEL] = the new level); hash 3931747190)
  • [TEMP] rival title (placeholder text; hash 1755574220)

Titles are shown exactly as the game spells them, including its typos: Kommisar next to Kommissar, Ålnäset next to Ålnästet, Överby bridge next to Överby Bridge, 3 titles with a doubled of of (counted under the right place here), and 4 titles with a trailing space (all Sävered). 5 places appear both with and without the (Boo Bridge, Töresta Coast, Viskandet Castle, Östertörn Bridge, Överby Air Base), so the 128 distinct place strings stand for fewer real places.
