Rivals

Rivals are machines that have grown stronger and earned a name and a title, such as The Tyrant of the Mountain. They came with the free Rivals & Experimental Weapons update (Oct 2019). When you destroy many machines in a region, the machines there adapt and one of them evolves into a regional rival; a machine that defeats you or a friend can become a personal rival. Each rival level multiplies its health, damage and reward, and rivals are where experimental weapons come from.

Rival levels

A rival's level follows from its experience points (XP). XP to reach is the total XP at which the level starts; the multipliers apply to the machine's normal values, and Reward multiplies what you get for destroying it.

  • Even a brand-new level 1 rival is tougher than the normal machine: ×1.5 health and ×1.25 damage. Take Hunter (FNIX), layout 2: its components add up to 2,099 hit points; as a level 4 rival that becomes about 6,298.
  • The total XP needed doubles with each level from level 2 on (576, 1,152, 2,304).
  • Rival multipliers come on top of difficulty: a level 4 rival on Hard with 4 players hits ×2 × 4 = ×8 as hard as base (see Difficulty & co-op).

Threat levels25 levels

Every region of the map keeps a threat score. The score sets the region's threat level, and a higher level raises the chance that a new rival appears there (and, according to the Gen0 Editor's notes, makes the region's rival missions stronger). The chart shows the score each level starts at and the new-rival chance at that level.

  • Level 1 starts at 0 points with a 0% chance; level 2 starts at 5% and every level after it adds 2.5 percentage points, up to 62.5% at level 25.
  • The steps get longer: level 2 needs 192 points, the last level 3,664 more than the one before.
From the game files
The game files give the thresholds and chances only. The Rivals update's Steam description says rivals appear as you take down large numbers of machines in a region; exactly how many points an action adds or removes isn't part of the extracted data.

Rival titles1,394 titles

Every rival gets a title from a table of 1,394 titles in the game files. Most follow one pattern:

TheEpithetof / fromPlace or people
  • 1,184 place titles combine 41 epithets (Tsar, Invader, Plague, Liberator ...) with 128 places, from map locations like Hagaboda to landscape words like the Rugged Wilds. Not every combination exists: each epithet appears with 24 to 48 places, and 27 titles use from (The Menace from Sävered).
  • 62 people titles name people instead of a place: The Bane of Friends, The Predator of Meatbags (32 epithets, 13 words for people). Their wording fits the personal rivals that have defeated players, but the files we read don't say when each set is used.
  • 148 stand-alone titles: The Red Savior, General Frost, Koschei the Deathless. Many borrow names from Russian folklore and myth (Baba Yaga, Koschei, Perun, the Zmey dragon, the Kikimora and the bogatyr heroes), which gives them a Soviet theme.

Texts in the title table that are not titles4 texts

Four texts in the title table are not titles: the rival history entries the game uses when a rival is created and levels up, one more that looks like a history entry, and a placeholder.

  • Became [TITLE] (history entry when the rival is created; its title fills in [TITLE])
  • Defeated a player (probably a history entry; not referenced in the game's history table)
  • Evolved to level [LEVEL] (history entry for each level gained; [LEVEL] is the new level)
  • [TEMP] rival title (placeholder text)
From the game files
Titles are shown exactly as the game spells them, including its typos: Kommisar next to Kommissar, Ålnäset next to Ålnästet, Överby bridge next to Överby Bridge, 3 titles with a doubled of of (counted under the right place here), and 4 titles with a trailing space (all Sävered). 5 places appear both with and without the (Boo Bridge, Töresta Coast, Viskandet Castle, Östertörn Bridge, Överby Air Base), so the 128 distinct place strings stand for fewer real places.
