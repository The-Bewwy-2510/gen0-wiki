The Vulture is the unique Rival-class Firebird. Besides a rocket pod and a flamethrower it drops beacons, gas canisters and turrets from its bomb bays.

Internally it is the firebird in class classr1, a Firebird of the Rival class. Its total component health is 52,150.

From the game files

Total component health: All component and armour hit points added up. The game keeps no separate overall health bar value in a readable file, so use this as a size comparison, not an exact kill threshold.

Heads up

All 4 Firebird classes in the files (Scout, Soldier, Spetsnaz, Rival) have exactly the same senses: a higher class does not see or hear you any farther (unaware, 150 m standing and 189 m hearing you run). See detection & stealth.

Weak points

  • Cheapest components to break: Fuel Tank (1,000 HP ×2), Part (1,000 HP ×4), Rocket Pod / Flamethrower (1,000 HP).
  • Weapons are components you can shoot off: Bomb Bay / Cluster Mines 4,250 HP ×2, Rocket Pod / Flamethrower 1,000 HP.
  • Fuel tanks: 1,000 HP ×2, a classic weak spot to aim for.
  • 13 armour plates hold 25% of the total (13,150 HP); the thinnest are 875 HP. Break plates with ammo that has a high armour multiplier, then switch to ammo with a high component multiplier for the parts underneath (see choosing ammo).

Detection

Unaware, it notices you at up to 150 m standing, 100 m crouched and 60 m prone (running: 200 m). Once alerted, sight grows to 2,520 m standing. It hears you running at 189 m and walking at 126 m. It starts searching at a detection score of 1, attacks at 1.9, and the score drains by 0.25 per second; at night sight is multiplied by 0.75. Full senses table.
