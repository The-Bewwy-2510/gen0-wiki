Wolf

The Wolf is the heavy Soviet ground machine: a gatling gun, missile pods and neurotoxin, and on some layouts a field that heals the machines around it.

It comes in 3 classes with 6 distinct layouts between them; total component health ranges from 6,037.5 to 12,157.7.

Weak points

  • Cheapest components to break: Battery (122.5 HP ×2), Core Drive (122.5 HP ×2), Radiator (122.5 HP).

Detection

Unaware, it notices you at up to 84 m standing, 36.8 m crouched and 15.8 m prone (running: 115.5 m). Once alerted, sight grows to 840 m standing. It hears you running at 31.5 m and walking at 21 m. Its vision cone is 90° wide. It starts searching at a detection score of 1, attacks at 1.9, and the score drains by 0.25 per second; at night sight is multiplied by 0.75. Full senses table.
