The Wolf is the heavy Soviet ground machine: a gatling gun, missile pods and neurotoxin, and on some layouts a field that heals the machines around it.

The game files call it wolf. It comes in 3 classes with 6 distinct layouts between them; total component health ranges from 6,037.5 to 12,157.7.

Detection

Unaware, it notices you at up to 84 m standing, 36.75 m crouched and 15.75 m prone (running: 115.5 m). Once alerted, sight grows to 840 m standing. It hears you running at 31.5 m and walking at 21 m. It starts searching at a detection score of 1, attacks at 1.9, and the score drains by 0.25 per second; at night sight is multiplied by 0.75. Full senses table.
