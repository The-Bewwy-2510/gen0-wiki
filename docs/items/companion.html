The companion

The companion is a Runner machine rebuilt to fight on your side. It travels with you, carries some of your gear, can wear armor and does tricks.

Getting the companion

You build it in the main mission Making Friends (Archipelago, story arc Answers). You collect three parts: the Core Unit at Källviken Beach, the Disabled Military Transceiver at Skarven Bunker and the Blank Disk Storage Drive at Dyviks Udde Ferry Pier. Then you go to the companion station next to the Vesslan bunker, assemble it there and deploy it. The last steps of the mission have you check its inventory, let it do a trick and build armor for it.

Companion Schematic Points (CSP)

Acquired by traveling with your Companion and destroying machines together.

Currency hash 1119011802

Armor24 schematics · 3,100 CSP

Companion armor is crafted from the schematics of the companion tree: six kinds, each in four qualities (Standard, Improved, Superior, Exceptional; the armor items themselves carry the name without the quality word and stack to 2). Each row is one quality: the schematic, what it costs, the points for the whole row up to it, the materials for one piece and the item it makes. The line under each kind says what it is for, going by its name and how it plays: the game files don't hold the armor's protection values, only its name.

  • Standard Runner Armor is free; every other kind starts at 30 CSP.
  • Taking one kind all the way to Exceptional costs 350 CSP for Runner Armor and 550 CSP for each of the others, so pick the kind you want before spending.
  • Each kind uses its own pair of materials, and the amount grows with quality: Runner Armor: Steel + Wood; Stealth Cladding Armor: Textile + Ethanol; Aggressive Threat Plate Armor: Accelerant + Refined Tungsten Alloy; Ballistic Plate Armor: Lead + Rubber; Concussive Plate Armor: Aluminum + Plastic; Firebreak Armor: Titanium + Copper.
