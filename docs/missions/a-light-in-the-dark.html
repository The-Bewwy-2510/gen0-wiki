Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Last week, Åsa and Patrik, two survivors in Anita's group, spotted some sort of light signal coming from the east while they were on a supply run. When the same pattern was repeated every day at the exact same time for a few days, the group was able to identify the light pattern as a distress call in Morse code and pinpoint its likely origin: the Klacksundsfyren lighthouse, on the far east of Himfjäll.

According to the message, a family of 9 has been surviving inside the lighthouse and are now desperate for help. Go to the lighthouse to meet up with the Olssons and try to convince them to join your group in the hotel.
