Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

As all of you suspected, it didn't take long for FNIX to understand your goal and put a stop to your plans. The cannon started aiming for the truck instead and nearly missed it, but the blast was close enough to damage a tire.

While Calle has to stay with the truck in the middle of hostile territory, far away from any other living soul, you need to look for a spare tire and repair the truck as quickly as possible before FNIX blasts it to pieces.
