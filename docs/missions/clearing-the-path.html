Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Calle's truck is now armored and loaded with C4, and thanks to Veronika's hacking device, you've located the approximate position of FNIX's cannon: the far end of the Hisinganäset peninsula (South Coast region). Calle insisted that he should be the one driving the truck for this perilous journey, while you will need to escort it through FNIX's front lines.

Defend the truck against enemy attacks, and make sure there are no major obstacles in its path.
