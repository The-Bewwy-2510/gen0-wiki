Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

The signal intelligence recovered from the Bertil Ludvig reconnaissance team at Iboholmen Castle indicated some kind of signal source at Källviken, a small beach located south of the castle ruins.

Pontus and Therese believe that this isolated shore may have been the origin point of the machines that roam the whole Archipelago Region. Investigating the area could provide clues about how the machine invasion took place.

Objectives4 required

The objectives in the order of the mission log. After names the objectives that have to be done first, opens with the ones that become available at the same time (they can be done in any order); ×N is how many times the game counts the objective. Some objectives also wait on hidden script steps (cut scenes, radio calls, spawns) that are not listed, so a step without an after may still have to wait for one of those.

  1. Follow the coordinates to the beach of Källviken
  2. Inspect the containers and look for any clues about their origin
  3. Locate the source of the enemy signal at Källviken
  4. Destroy the non-active machines at Källviken
