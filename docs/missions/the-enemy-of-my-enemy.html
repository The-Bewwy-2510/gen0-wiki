Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Now that all the jamming relay towers are destroyed, you've finally been able to establish a clear radio transmission.
A Russian official introducing himself as Sergei Kuznetsov informed you that both the Soviet Union and NATO are aware of the situation in Sweden and offer its assistance. They can provide a device that should disable whatever is controlling the machines, and the Russian navy will deliver it to shore for you to pick up, together with the necessary intelligence.

Go to Ålnästet, an ancient estate west of Hagaboda, on the northern shore of Myrdalen in the Marshlands, and pick up the device to put an end to this.
