Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Elsa has some knowledge on FNIX as well as the reason why the Apocalypse Class machines seem to roam Himfjäll in much greater numbers than anywhere else: according to her, their point of origin is located quite close to Björntunet. To Anita, Benny, and the rest of the group, who had no idea what they've been up against until then, her revelations are a shock.

Use Elsa's intel to locate the research facility and destroy the enemy at the core.
