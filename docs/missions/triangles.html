Briefing

The unreleased mission log text from the files.

As you entered the Marshlands, you realize that the whole region is dominated by the Soviets, who managed to push back FNIX's forces, advance deep into Östertörn and establish a front line in the southern border of the Marshlands.
What is their objective? Why are they both engaging FNIX's machines and chasing down survivors?
A former electrician specialized in electrical demolition, Janek will build a radio tracker emulating the machines' GLONASS navigation system in order to locate the source of the invasion. It's the only way to get to the bottom of this.
