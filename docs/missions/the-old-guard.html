Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

A transcribed radio transmission mentions a group of old men who, not complying with the evacuation orders, engaged and apparently locked in a number of enemies inside a readiness storage using one of their weapons.

Go there and destroy the enemies, and find anything more about what they were up to.

Objectives3 required

The objectives in the order of the mission log.
