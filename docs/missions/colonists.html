Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

In the early hours of the cataclysm, a farmer and his nephew managed to trap what they describe as "an alien colonist" in his barn at the farm of Stora Dyrbo, north of the military airfield and get out of there alive. Unfortunately for them, their emergency supplies were also left in there.

Objectives2 required · 1 optional

The objectives in the order of the mission log. After names the objectives that have to be done first, opens with the ones that become available at the same time (they can be done in any order); ×N is how many times the game counts the objective.
