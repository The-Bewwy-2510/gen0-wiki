Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

A shipping manifest found near a readiness storage of the Swedish Army refers to a warehouse in Nyhamnen, Östervik's new port. The manifest was produced by the Accounting Office East, belonging to the Swedish National Defense Research Institute (FOA).

Any other specifics as to the contents of that warehouse and the shipments sent there have been redacted. It is likely that some of the machines have activated there.
