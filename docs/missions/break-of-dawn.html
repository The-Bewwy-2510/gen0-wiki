Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Upon arrival back at Iboholmen after your group's weekend excursion on an island, it becomes immediately clear that something is not right. Something destroyed your boat, with you barely making it onto shore alive. You are injured and defenseless.

Whatever it was, the thing that attacked your boat seems to have moved on, but the place seems eerily abandoned. Maybe there are people hiding in the nearby house that can tell you a bit more about what is going on.
