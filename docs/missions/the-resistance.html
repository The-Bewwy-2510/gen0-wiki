Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

FNIX will soon send waves after waves of enemies to destroy the Björntunet Hotel and eliminate all the survivors. Why, how, since when... all of those questions about the enemy don't matter as much now. Anita and the others count on you to defend the hotel for as long as you can, so that you can all make it through another day.
