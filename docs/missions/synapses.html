Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

The newest addition to the Ringfort group, Fredrik, recently received a transmission in Morse code from a certain Holberg. It contained information about "neural nodes," the cable nodes used by FNIX and how they could be tampered with. It also mentioned the three locations where they were sighted: Villa Solhöjden, Tokeröd Farm and Fort Torsberga.

Maybe you can use Veronika's hacking device on them to override the machines' behaviors to your advantage.
