Challenges

Challenges are Generation Zero's long-term goals: 34 challenges in 6 trees, each a chain of tiers (132 tiers in all). Every tier you complete awards a title for your character. This page lists every challenge with its tiers, the exact numbers the game counts and the title of each tier, straight from the game files.

How to read the tiers

A challenge keeps one progress counter for all its tiers. From → to is the counter at the start and end of a tier, so Needed (to minus from) is how far that tier moves the counter, and the last tier's to is the challenge's total. For most challenges that is what the tier asks for. A few (Combat Perfectionist, Eagle Eye and the ones that name missions) count completed tiers instead, one per tier; there the requirement text gives the real number, and where it asks for more than one kill Needed shows that number with tier +1. The tiers come one after another and each has its own condition: later tiers often ask for a tougher machine class (for example "Military or FNIX class only") or a harder feat than the first ones.

From the game files
The tier descriptions are the game's own text. Where they say "the last N missions" of a region, the total is what the challenge counts; the region may hold more missions than that today, since the expansions and later updates added missions. The descriptions don't say whether those count, so we list both numbers below.
