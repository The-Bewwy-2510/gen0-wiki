Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

You and Calle are almost there: you made it to the core site, and the massive cannon that FNIX has been using to lay waste on the Måsskär camp and the Ringfort is right there in front of you, at the top of the hill.

However, the cannon targeted the truck once more, and this time it was a direct hit. The truck took heavy damage, but was miraculously not destroyed. The last obstacle between the cannon and the C4 in the back of the truck are three gates: open them to allow Calle to drive the truck up next to the cannon, and arm the explosives to blow it up to pieces once and for all.
