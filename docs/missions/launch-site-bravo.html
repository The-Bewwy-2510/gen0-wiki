Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Klas's memories from inside the machine network led you to the Norrmyra Artillery Range where you discovered a destroyed rocket launch silo. The rocket appeared to be configured to launch the prefrontal core, the most central control component of the FNIX network, into space aboard a satellite where it would be unreachable.

Documents you found in the launch control room revealed the existence of launch site Bravo, somewhere on “Observatory Island” west of the Mountains region, which FNIX is likely using to continue its space launch plans.

Veronika, Tatiana, and Klas have gone ahead to scout the island.
