Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Before finding refuge in the tunnels of the Ringfort to escape a swarm of machines, Veronika hid for a few days in a cellar at Ängsäs Manor, on the west coast in the Sävered Region, together with an Air Force Vråken fighter pilot called Elsa Khorsandi. Soon afterwards, they got separated and never saw each other again; but thanks to you, Veronika was delighted to find out that Elsa is still alive and safe in Hotel Björntunet on the island of Himfjäll. Maybe Elsa left a clue for Veronika in that cellar?

Veronika explained how FNIX's Ticks were busy putting up walls and connecting a lot of mysterious electrical devices. She suspects those might be supercomputers based on the Gray models they had at FOA 53; maybe FNIX is setting up data centers of sorts to increase its computational and storage capacity? In any case, destroying them would be a "thorn in FNIX's side", to use her words.

She warned you that there are a lot of machines guarding the location, so FNIX is clearly deeming this a key structure to protect. So this time around, maybe she recommended you use stealth to infiltrate the facility, rather than engage the machines head-on.
