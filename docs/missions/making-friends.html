Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

A notebook page describes a theory of a way to assemble a Runner. Is it possible to make one into a loyal companion? The creator of the note left it near the Vesslan bunker for anyone bold enough to pick up the trail. Investigate if the theory is true or if it is just a rumor.

Three locations were listed with a blue marker.

  • Källviken Beach
  • Skarven Bunker
  • Dyviks Udde Ferry Pier
