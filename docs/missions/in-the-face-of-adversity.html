Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

One of the handful of people that did not die in the bombing of the Måsskär camp, Jenny, used to volunteer for supply runs. She and her twin sister Anna, both retired professional racing cyclists, would race against each other, as a way to have some fun despite the tragic circumstances.

Two days before Måsskär fell, Jenny's sister left to drop off some supplies in a container, for any other group of survivors to find and take, but never returned. Her destination was Åsötungan, the narrow patch of land connecting the Archipelago region to the rest of Östertörn. Jenny asked you to look for her, and challenged you to take part in their competition.

To do so, all you need is to go to the bridge near Lillå (a neighborhood to the southwest of Östervik) and get a bike.
