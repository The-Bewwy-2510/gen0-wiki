Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

During his journey before joining the other survivors at the Ringfort, Fredrik identified a machine convoy patrolling near the village of Kålleby. Based on his assumptions, Harvesters deal with the enemy's logistical needs - but some of it would make a decent defense stash to ensure the Ringfort's survival.
