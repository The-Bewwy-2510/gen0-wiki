Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

A letter found in a car involved in an accident on the island of Iboholmen was apparently sent to a hunter named Kurt Ahlgren, who lives in a location called "Björknäs", also on Iboholmen. Your map features a wooded area called "Björknässkogen" (literally, "Björknäs woods"), so his house is probably nearby. With a bit of luck, the owner's hunting equipment will still be at their house.
