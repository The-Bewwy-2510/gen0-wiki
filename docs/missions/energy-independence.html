Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Fredrik, the latest addition to the group, did not live on Måsskär like the other Ringfort dwellers. He tends to keep to himself a lot, unless it's important to their survival. He hasn't let on much about his life either, except that he managed to sneak past enemy lines in the South Coast and observe them for a while.

He noticed tall, newly-built structures that he assumes are some kind of power generators. Maybe FNIX is trying to achieve energy independence and sustainability - and if that's the case, those generators have to be destroyed.
