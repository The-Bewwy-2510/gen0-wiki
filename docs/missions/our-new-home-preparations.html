Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

[Home Base Defense - Preparation stage]
The Command Center at the Tylöveden Home Base is the very first one you've helped the Resistance set up; there is no doubt FNIX will soon launch a large-scale assault to nip that hope in the bud.
Thankfully, its long-range radio station now allows you to receive warnings from other survivor groups about incoming attacks.

Once you are geared up and ready for a massive battle, choose which warning signal you'll receive, which will influence the scale of FNIX's assault on your Home Base in this session.
