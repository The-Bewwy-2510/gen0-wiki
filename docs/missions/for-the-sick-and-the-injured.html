Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

You found Roland in the café at Marksätern, but like the rest of the group at Lennart's Marin, he too was killed by the "Apocalypse Class" Runners that roam Himfjäll. They were looking for any medicine they could get their hands on (painkillers, sleeping pills, disinfectant and so on), to offer much-needed relief to some of the other survivors.

Find medicine and bring it to Anita's group to gain their trust and meet up with them.

Objectives7 required

The objectives in the order of the mission log. After names the objectives that have to be done first, opens with the ones that become available at the same time (they can be done in any order); ×N is how many times the game counts the objective. Some objectives also wait on hidden script steps (cut scenes, radio calls, spawns) that are not listed, so a step without an after may still have to wait for one of those.

  1. Find a map of Himfjäll
  2. Find your way to the town of Himarvetafter 1
  3. Locate the Aca supermarket in Himarvetafter 2
  4. Look for medicine (and soda...) for Anita's group×2
  5. Locate and reach Björntunet Hotel
  6. Secure the ground floor of the Björntunet Hotel×4
  7. Meet up with Anita's group in the wine cellar of the Björntunetafter 6
