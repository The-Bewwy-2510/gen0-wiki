Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

While you were raiding the Himarvet police station for weapons and ammo, your walkie-talkie picked up a garbled transmission by an unidentified Vråken pilot. Is it the remnants of the Swedish Army starting to fight back? Try to establish communication with the pilot, so that she can maybe join forces with you and Anita's group.
