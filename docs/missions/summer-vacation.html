Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Kenneth is the young man that recently found shelter in the Ringfort tunnels, and one of the only survivors of the Måsskär camp. He and Alexander Larsson were cousins, and had managed to survive and stick together until they were found by Calle and Freddie, and brought to Måsskär.

After Alexander left on a supply run never to return, Kenneth wishes to find one of their childhood memories, in the hope to return it to Alexander in person some day, if he's still alive out there.
