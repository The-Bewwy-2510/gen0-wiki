Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

At last, you've found proof that there are other survivors out there. North of the IGA Industrial Facility in the Forest Region, there's a convoy that was dispatched by the Swedish Army in the direct aftermath of Cataclysm Day; in one of the trucks, a computer terminal connected to a radio picked up a looping message of Anita Sjögren calling for survivors.

She seems to be with a small group somewhere on Himfjäll, a ski resort island located east of Östervik, and her group is trying to set up a makeshift boat passage to and from the island.
