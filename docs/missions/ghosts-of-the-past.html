Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

You found a mysterious message displayed on a computer screen in the Warboard room of the Bergfinken research facility. It is now clear that the enemy knows about Anita's group and is planning a large-scale offensive on the hotel.
You don't know how long you have left until they start attacking. The only thing you have time for is to try and make the hotel a little safer and easier to defend.
