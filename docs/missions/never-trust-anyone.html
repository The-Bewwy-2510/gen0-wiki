Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Uncle Calle told you that a "buddy" of his had a house at Överbytorp, a stone's throw to the east of the Ringfort. He called her Old Bodil, and both of them were spending a lot of time together talking about aliens, military conspiracies and government cover-ups and interpreting news to fit their beliefs.

He also mentioned that Bodil was a prepper and was good at sewing, especially when it came to make clothes sturdier - "just in case the world goes to shit", she always said.
You might find the schematic and materials you need to craft something for yourself, but also craft a few more for the Ringfort dwellers.
