Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

The Archipelago is connected to the rest of Östertörn through a single road and a narrow patch of land called Åsötungan. This was one of humankind's last stands, weeks after the Cataclysm of November 10th, 1989, and the violent, but extremely short war that ensued between the machines and the Swedish Armed Forces.

However, all hope is not lost yet: in that lost outpost at Åsötungan, there's a recent clue to the location of a large survivor camp; the message was sent from an "Uncle Calle".

Are there still pockets of resistance on Östertörn after all?
