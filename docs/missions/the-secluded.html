Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

A mysterious, secluded community called Ljuset Commune (literally, "the Commune of Light") used to live not far from the Björntunet Hotel.
Anita's group would like you to go to their settlement and check up on them. What if their relative isolation allowed them to remain undetected? Would they be willing to cooperate with other groups?
