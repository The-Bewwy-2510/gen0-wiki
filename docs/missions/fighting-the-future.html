Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

After she was chased out of her most recent hideout at Ängsäs Manor and before she found refuge in the tunnels, Veronika caught a glimpse of the massive changes FNIX was doing to the world: the machines were building outposts on top of the two highest hills in the Sävered region: Säveredsberg, west of the farm of Knäperanna, and Ängsnäsberg, near Brevikens Camping.

It's still unclear what they were building or why, but based on how well defended they are, the structures they hide are clearly critical to FNIX's exponential growth plans. Now is the time to cripple it to prevent the future that FNIX intends to build from becoming a reality.
