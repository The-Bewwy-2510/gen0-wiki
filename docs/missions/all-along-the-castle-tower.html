Briefing

The unreleased mission log text from the files.

Janek just caught a Morse code message from an unidentified source stating that a Soviet EMP weapon prototype had been lost at a castle ruin.
EMP is the perfect weapon type against machines, because they are damaging to electronics, but have less effect on humans.
The previous Soviet platoon drove here in an MCV that carried the EMP weapon and assembled it there. Their goal was to use the weapon they brought with them at a strategic location, lure as many machines as they could, and disable them.
They failed, so you need to find it and take over where they left off.
