Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

You found two bodies dead next to each other on one of the small islands off the shore near the hunting club at Bergahyttan. They seemed to be false twins, and were wearing the same outfit - worn jeans, a checkered wool shirt, and sturdy outdoor shoes. They were both shot by a single shot to the back of the head at point blank, which seems to indicate that they were killed before the Cataclysm.
Investigate the clues to find out more about the twins and solve the double murder.
