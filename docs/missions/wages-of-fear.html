Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

You managed to repair the truck, but now it's clear that FNIX will stop at nothing to destroy it. It has now shifted its focus and will target the truck, not you. The truck's armor will absorb some of the damage and protect Calle (and the C4 that is loaded in the back) for a short while, but it won't sustain heavy fire for long.

There is no turning back now. This is the last stretch before you and Calle can reach the core site where FNIX's cannon is located.
