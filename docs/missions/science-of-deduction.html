Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

According to a logged transmission at the Hermelinen Command Center, there is a facility nearby used by department 5 of the Defense Research Institute (FOA), that experienced an emergency lockdown in the morning hours before the attack.

The affected sub-department called FOA 53 has seemingly recently had its entire research team murdered or gone missing on the same night, just a few days before the cataclysm. Everything points to the facility in question being somehow linked to the machine invasion. Go there to find out more.
