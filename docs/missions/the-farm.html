Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

You have met up with Therese, a tired and rattled teenager who barricaded herself in the organ loft at Iboholmen Church to escape the mysterious machines. After you helped her tend to her wounds, she mentions that her grandparents have disappeared, along with apparently everyone in the area.

She suggests to keep following the road west to look for help; together, maybe she and you can find some answers as well.
