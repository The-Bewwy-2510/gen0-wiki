Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Unlocks Base Building and Base Assault

A report was sent to Vesslan from a group of soldiers who had seen signs of machines building something in the Farmlands. A squad from Vesslan was dispatched to rendezvous with the soldiers and get further information. They should have reported back, but have not been heard from since.

You should check the patrol office and see where the squad from Vesslan went. If the machines really are building something, someone needs to stop them before it’s too late.
