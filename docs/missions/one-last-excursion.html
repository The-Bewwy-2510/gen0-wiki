Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

The Resistance team that’s been investigating Soviet machines in the Marshlands have eyes on a Soviet soldier who might have important information about the Soviet machines written down in a journal. They were planning to ambush the soldier and take the journal.

However, the Resistance team never came back from their planned ambush. Someone needs to find out what happened and report it back to the Östervik Resistance.

Objectives9 required · 2 optional

The objectives in the order of the mission log.
