Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Home Base Defense
Difficulty level: Hard
Note: This is a replayable mission, and can be replayed any number of times once it is completed or failed, as long as you meet the requirements to unlock it again.

You have managed to create the humans' first "Home Base", a place that can be defended and reinforced, in order to protect a Command Center doubling as a long-range radio station. Now survivors can try to get in contact with other groups, and start trading goods, arranging supply runs and sharing knowledge.

Without it, humans will lose all hope to become a match for FNIX someday, and FNIX knows it. It will throw its forces against your Home Base and raze it to the ground. No matter how many times FNIX tries to destroy the home base, you will be there to defeat the incoming waves of machines and defend the Command Center at all costs.

It's time to turn things around and lay the first brick towards building the "Resistance" Veronika and Elsa have been striving for.
