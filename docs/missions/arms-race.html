Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

The survivors holed up in the wine cellar of the Björntunet Hotel are not safe. They've cleverly managed to stay hidden for a few weeks since the Cataclysm, but they're close to running out on ammo. Sooner or later, the enemy will find and exterminate them.

Thanks to one of the survivors, police officer Åsa Jefferson, they've known the police station in the town of Himarvet contains a handful of weapons and a bit of ammo, but until now, they didn't have any skilled and able fighters to power through enemy lines. Now that you're in the picture, they count on you to carve a safe way there for them and help defend themselves. And in the process you too might gain some firepower against the advanced machines that seem to be prevalent on the island.
