Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Local reporter Monika Byström had come to be renowned for her hard-hitting, uncompromising articles, and had recently started investigating a Fredrik Holberg, a high-ranking military official of whom little is known to the public.
Her sources converge towards a military compound located on the western mountain of Himfjäll. Is there anything left there to discover about what happened?
