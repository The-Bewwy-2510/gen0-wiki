Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

There seems to be a coastal artillery fortress called Fort Torsberga in the Hisinganäset peninsula, in the South Coast Region. It appears it was one of the first locations to come under attack and was of utmost strategic importance to the Swedish Army. Find out why and thwart whatever plans the enemy had for it.
