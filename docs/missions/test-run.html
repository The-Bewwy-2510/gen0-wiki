Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

You scavenged armor parts off Rival machines to reinforce Calle's old truck. Before you can start your journey to destroy FNIX's massive cannon, the last thing you need is a way for the truck to push any obstacles out of the way... as well as intel on the location of the cannon.

Unexpectedly, FNIX downloaded itself into one of the Rivals, called EBBA, and addressed you directly on your own walkie-talkie frequency. Much to her shock and confusion, Veronika recognized that voice: Dr. Svante Von Ulmer, her former Research Director at FOA 53.

Is the man she had always looked up to behind the Cataclysm and all that happened in the last few weeks - the population vanishing, the countless deaths, this new world?
