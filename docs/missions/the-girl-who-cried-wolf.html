Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

An FOA computer engineer named Veronika Nilsson was in contact with an officer at the Minken Command Center. Days before the attack she tried to warn them that something bad was about to happen, but the officer didn't take her seriously.

On the day of the cataclysm, she tried to get in contact with Minken again, connecting the evacuation to a mysterious series of murders that took place just a few days earlier. She left an address in the fishing village of Kålleby, located in the north-west of the South Coast Region.
