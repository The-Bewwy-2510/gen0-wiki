Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

The Command Bunker Hermelinen was monitoring communication from the Hagaboda police department on the morning of the attack and picked up a notification of a patrol car going out to the Hagaboda radio mast to investigate disruption in the police radio communication.

This is likely one of the radio masts used by the enemy to prevent communication with the outside world.
