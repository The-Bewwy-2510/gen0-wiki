Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

It seems that the Mården Command Center intercepted an emergency call on the day of the invasion from a nearby gas factory, as they were being attacked. Everything points to gas having been used by the machines, so maybe this factory is where they got it?

It is not known if the army sent out help or not, but it might be worth investigating the facility. It appears to be located somewhere east of Östervik.
