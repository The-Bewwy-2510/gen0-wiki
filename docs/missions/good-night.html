Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

Instead of placing the C4 on the cannon as planned, Calle decided to sacrifice himself by driving the truck loaded with C4 head-on into the cannon, to make sure it would blow up to pieces. Was that his plan from the start? He was successful, but Veronika is filled with grief and anger against FNIX.

All there is left to do now is maybe to rescue survivors: are some of the kidnapped people really in this facility? What is FNIX using them for? And the most personal question for Veronika: is FNIX really the same being as Svante?

But before you and Veronika can finally get some answers, you need to defeat a Rival named "EBBA" - the same name used by one of the Rivals you encountered previously in the Farmlands.
