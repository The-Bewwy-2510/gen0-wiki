Briefing

The mission log text, as the game shows it.

According to the emergency procedures you found in the computer in the Civil defense office, the people of Salthamn and Iboholmen were gathered for further evacuation on the Saltvallen sports field west of the town. But where were they evacuated, and why?

Therese has decided to stay in the bomb shelter for her own safety, but is determined to help you however she can over walkie-talkie; in the meantime, you will look for answers about what happened to Östertörn since November 10th 1989.
