Collectibles

Generation Zero hides 204 collectibles in 15 series around Östertörn and Himfjäll, from garden gnomes and Dala horses to mixtapes, machine blueprints and audio logs. This section also covers the schematic point pickups and the house photos of the safehouses: 369 entries in all, 362 of them with an exact position. Pick a type for its map and the place of every pickup.

Tips

  • Counting only the collectible series, Forest holds the most: 38 of the 197 collectibles with a map position.
  • Some series stay in one region, so you can finish them in one trip: Audio Logs (Himfjäll), Character Biographies (Himfjäll), Vacation Photos (Himfjäll), Love Letters (Farmlands), Resistance Bottles (Forest), Newspapers (Forest), Soviet Dolls (Marshlands) and Toy Soldiers (North Coast).
  • The interactive map shows every collectible at once; each type page links to a map with only that type.
  • What's The Skinny counts pickups that are on the base game map, but the challenge itself is part of FNIX Rising.
  • House photos sit inside their safehouse, so you pick them up as you unlock safehouses.
Audio log count
The map data has 18 audio log markers, but there are only 9 audio logs: every log is also placed in a dialogue scene at one shared spot. Lists that count 18 audio logs count each one twice.
