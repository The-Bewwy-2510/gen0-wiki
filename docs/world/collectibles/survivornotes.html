Survivor's Notes

Notes left behind by survivors, counted together with the Love Letters by the challenge What's The Skinny. The game files place 10 across 2 regions, the most in South Coast (9). They lie on the base game map, but the challenge that counts them, What's The Skinny, is part of the FNIX Rising expansion.
