Dalahäst

Dala horses (dalahäst), the carved and painted wooden horse that is the classic Swedish souvenir, each with its own paint job from Classic to Skeleton and Kronor. The game files place 14 across 7 regions, the most in Farmlands, Forest and Marshlands (3 each).
