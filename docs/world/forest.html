The town of Östervik with its old town, harbour and neighbourhoods, surrounded by forest, lumber yards, hunting camps and readiness storage bunkers.

The Forest region lies in the north-east of the map. The game files place 48 locations here, most of them settlements, control points, farms, with 12 safehouses, 8 control points and 14 missions.

From the game files
Two crate numbers. The crate spawn points are the CLootCrateSpawnPoint objects listed in the location files: what your save tracks for a location's loot progress. The crate spots (map layer) are every crate spot of the interactive map's loot layer in this region, whichever game file it comes from, so the two numbers rarely match. Neither means a crate is always there: the game may leave a spot empty. This region's location files list 2,718 distinct crate spawn points.
