Two crate numbers. The crate spawn points are the CLootCrateSpawnPoint objects listed in the location files: what your save tracks for a location's loot progress. The crate spots (map layer) are every crate spot of the interactive map's loot layer within 200 m that is nearer to this location than to any other, whichever game file it comes from, so the two numbers rarely match. Neither means a crate is always there: the game may leave a spot empty.
