World

The world

Generation Zero is set in Östertörn, a fictional part of Sweden, in 1989. The game files split the world into 8 regions with 359 named locations, 104 safehouses and 145 missions (6 of them unreleased, in the files only). Seven regions are on the mainland and its islands; the eighth, Himfjäll, comes with the Alpine Unrest expansion.

Map of the regions

The coloured outlines are the region borders from the map data; click a region to open its page. Each name sits at the middle of the region (the average position of its locations). Himfjäll has no border in the map data, so the dashed box simply frames its locations. For every marker, open the interactive map.

Control points include 3 Mountains markers (Angerås, Kilsberget, Rusaberget) that are not map locations, which is why the Locations by kind table below doesn't count them.
