The northern shore with the walled town of Hagaboda, the FOA Facility, castle ruins and the Soviet positions: command quarters, research lab and trenches.

The North Coast region lies in the north of the map. The game files place 28 locations here, most of them tourist attractions, soviet, campsites, with 11 safehouses, no control points and 11 missions.

From the game files
Two crate numbers. The crate spawn points are the CLootCrateSpawnPoint objects listed in the location files: what your save tracks for a location's loot progress. The crate spots (map layer) are every crate spot of the interactive map's loot layer in this region, whichever game file it comes from, so the two numbers rarely match. Neither means a crate is always there: the game may leave a spot empty. This region's location files list 1,447 distinct crate spawn points.
