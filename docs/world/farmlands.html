Base game

Open farm country around Överby, Boo and Dyrbo, with the F23 Överby Air Base, the Boo Bruk AB works, old stone ships and the densest ring of control points in the game.

The Farmlands region lies in the middle of the map. The game files place 68 locations here, most of them farms, control points, tourist attractions, with 19 safehouses, 12 control points and 22 missions.

From the game files
Two crate numbers. The crate spawn points are the CLootCrateSpawnPoint objects listed in the location files: what your save tracks for a location's loot progress. The crate spots (map layer) are every crate spot of the interactive map's loot layer in this region, whichever game file it comes from, so the two numbers rarely match. Neither means a crate is always there: the game may leave a spot empty. This region's location files list 3,485 distinct crate spawn points.
