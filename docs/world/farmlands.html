Region

Farmlands

Open farm country around Överby, Boo and Dyrbo, with the F23 Överby Air Base, the Boo Bruk AB works, old stone ships and the densest ring of control points in the game.

The Farmlands region lies in the middle of the map. The game files place 68 locations here, most of them farms, control points, tourist attractions, with 19 safehouses, 12 control points and 22 missions.
