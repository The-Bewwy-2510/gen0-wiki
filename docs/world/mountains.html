Region

Mountains

Rugged high ground with the village of Klinte and its harbour, the Redovisningskansli Öst military compound and its Muskudden port complex, and many cabins and mountain bunkers.

The Mountains region lies in the west of the map. The game files place 37 locations here, most of them farms, bunkers, campsites, with 13 safehouses, 3 control points and 13 missions.
