Base game

Rugged high ground with the village of Klinte and its harbour, the Redovisningskansli Öst military compound and its Muskudden port complex, and many cabins and mountain bunkers.

The Mountains region lies in the west of the map. The game files place 37 locations here, most of them farms, bunkers, campsites, with 13 safehouses, 3 control points and 13 missions.

From the game files
Two crate numbers. The crate spawn points are the CLootCrateSpawnPoint objects listed in the location files: what your save tracks for a location's loot progress. The crate spots (map layer) are every crate spot of the interactive map's loot layer in this region, whichever game file it comes from, so the two numbers rarely match. Neither means a crate is always there: the game may leave a spot empty. This region's location files list 2,660 distinct crate spawn points.
