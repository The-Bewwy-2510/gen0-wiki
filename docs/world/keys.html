How keys work

Keys, barcodes, keycards and mission items are not part of your backpack. The save keeps them in the profile's item stash (Profile.ItemStash), which every character of the profile shares, with an amount for each. The save has no record of opened doors: holding the key is what opens its door. Stack is how many of an item the game lets you hold (3 for the bunker barcode pieces, 1 for everything else). Items tagged Mission-flagged are flagged as mission items in the files: a mission normally hands them out or asks for them.

Pack is the content pack (DLC) the item belongs to. An item of a pack the account doesn't own is moved aside by the game (to InaccessibleItems) instead of being usable. Missions that name it quotes the mission line that asks for the item.

Kinds of keys

  • Command bunker barcodes (7): Each command bunker needs a full entry barcode, which the game splits into pieces: the files give every barcode a stack of 3. You collect the pieces from destroyed machines of that region (the Warboard missions say so), and a full barcode opens that region's command bunker. Activating the Warboards is the challenge The New Total Defense (activate the last 3 command bunker warboards for the last tier).
  • Facility barcodes and keycards (4): Barcodes and keycards for research facilities and their labs.
  • Door keys (22): Ordinary keys to houses, barns and storage rooms. Several open a door a mission asks you to get through; for the rest no mission text names them.
  • Mission items (10): Items a mission hands out or asks for. Most of them belong to FNIX Rising.
