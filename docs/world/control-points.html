Control points & beacons

Control points are the bases of the Resistance war against FNIX: places you capture, turn into a home base and defend. Beacons are the machines' own markers on the map: relay beacons (machine nests) you destroy, and the Soviet beacons on the North Coast. This page lists every one from the game files, with maps.

Control points30

A control point is a base you can take. Once it is yours it can become a home base: every control point has a player spawn point on top of it, and you can build defences there. The save records who holds each one: you and the Resistance, FNIX, or nobody (neutral). A control point FNIX holds is a FNIX base and has to be taken back.

Base building starts with the main mission Building Blocks, whose last steps are “Claim the Control Point” and “Start building your own base”. The defence missions of the Our New Home series take place at a home base.

By region: South Coast 7, Farmlands 12, Forest 8, Mountains 3. Archipelago, Marshlands, North Coast, Himfjäll (Alpine Unrest) have none.

The nearest location column helps you find each one; the nearest safehouse is where you would fast travel to before an attack.

Relay beacons70

Relay beacons are machine nests: machine-built transmitters that show on the map as Unknown signal interference. Destroying one disables the nest; the save then marks that nest location as switched off, and the Relay Beacons disabled stat goes up.

The challenge Kill Switch (Resistance tree) rewards destroying them: Destroy 5 Relay Beacons; Destroy 10 more Relay Beacons; Destroy 15 more Relay Beacons.

The map data has 70 relay beacon markers at 64 sites: markers within 100 m of each other are counted as one site below. 13 of the markers come from the bounty nest files (marked Bounty). The sites are listed by region, with the nearest location to help you find them.

Markers by region (sites in brackets where they differ): Archipelago 15 (9 sites), South Coast 6, Farmlands 13, Forest 9, Mountains 7, Marshlands 8, North Coast 6, Himfjäll (Alpine Unrest) 6.
