Alpine Unrest

Himfjäll, the mountain island of the Alpine Unrest expansion: resort villages, hotels and slope cabins, readiness storage bunkers, the Digerhällen Artillery Base and the FOA 4 bunker "Bergfinken".

The Himfjäll (Alpine Unrest) region lies in the east of the map. The game files place 39 locations here, most of them tourist attractions, settlements, military bases, with 9 safehouses, no control points and 20 missions. It is the Alpine Unrest region: you need that expansion to go there.

From the game files
Two crate numbers. The crate spawn points are the CLootCrateSpawnPoint objects listed in the location files: what your save tracks for a location's loot progress. The crate spots (map layer) are every crate spot of the interactive map's loot layer in this region, whichever game file it comes from, so the two numbers rarely match. Neither means a crate is always there: the game may leave a spot empty. This region's location files list 3,475 distinct crate spawn points.
