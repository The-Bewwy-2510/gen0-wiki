Himfjäll (Alpine Unrest)

Himfjäll, the mountain island of the Alpine Unrest expansion: resort villages, hotels and slope cabins, readiness storage bunkers, the Digerhällen Artillery Base and the FOA 4 bunker "Bergfinken".

The Himfjäll (Alpine Unrest) region lies in the east of the map. The game files place 39 locations here, most of them tourist attractions, settlements, military bases, with 9 safehouses, no control points and 20 missions. It is the Alpine Unrest region: you need that expansion to go there.
