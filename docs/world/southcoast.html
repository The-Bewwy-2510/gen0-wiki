The Hisinganäset peninsula and the Sävered area: fishing villages, farms and Torsberga Fort, and the heart of FNIX territory with its data centres, generators and cable nodes.

The South Coast region lies in the south-west of the map. The game files place 60 locations here, most of them fnix, farms, control points, with 19 safehouses, 7 control points and 23 missions.

From the game files
Two crate numbers. The crate spawn points are the CLootCrateSpawnPoint objects listed in the location files: what your save tracks for a location's loot progress. The crate spots (map layer) are every crate spot of the interactive map's loot layer in this region, whichever game file it comes from, so the two numbers rarely match. Neither means a crate is always there: the game may leave a spot empty. This region's location files list 3,031 distinct crate spawn points.
