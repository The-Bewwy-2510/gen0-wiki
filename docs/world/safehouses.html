Safehouses

Generation Zero has 104 safehouses in 8 regions. This page lists them all with their positions, the location they belong to and whether a house photo marks them. The starting safehouse is Yttervik in the Archipelago.

How safehouses work

  • An unlocked safehouse is a respawn point and a fast travel destination.
  • Your save keeps the list of unlocked safehouses (the Safe houses unlocked stat counts them). Yttervik is unlocked on a new character.
  • 91 of the 104 safehouses have a house photo collectible placed right next to them (within 50 m); the Himfjäll safehouses have none.
  • The Location column links the map location the safehouse sits in (same name, or within 150 m); hidden camps and caves are often not a named location of their own.
  • The coordinates open the interactive map on that safehouse.
From the game files
The game data lists 1 more start spawn point that is not one of the map's safehouses.
