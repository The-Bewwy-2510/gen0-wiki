The islands where the game begins: Iboholmen, where you come ashore at Yttervik, and Norra and Södra Saltholmen with the Saltholmen naval base, its bunkers and the Vesslan Command Bunker.

The Archipelago region lies in the south-east of the map. The game files place 41 locations here, most of them farms, tourist attractions, settlements, with 10 safehouses, no control points and 22 missions.

From the game files
Two crate numbers. The crate spawn points are the CLootCrateSpawnPoint objects listed in the location files: what your save tracks for a location's loot progress. The crate spots (map layer) are every crate spot of the interactive map's loot layer in this region, whichever game file it comes from, so the two numbers rarely match. Neither means a crate is always there: the game may leave a spot empty. This region's location files list 2,464 distinct crate spawn points.
