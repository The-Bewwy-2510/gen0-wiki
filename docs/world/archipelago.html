Region

Archipelago

The islands where the game begins: Iboholmen, where you come ashore at Yttervik, and Norra and Södra Saltholmen with the Saltholmen naval base, its bunkers and the Vesslan Command Bunker.

The Archipelago region lies in the south-east of the map. The game files place 41 locations here, most of them farms, tourist attractions, settlements, with 10 safehouses, no control points and 22 missions.
