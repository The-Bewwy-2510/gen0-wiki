Quality & experimental

Quality runs from Dilapidated (1 crown) to Special (5 crowns), plus Experimental for a few weapons. This page shows what each level changes and the damage multiplier of every weapon at every quality.

Quality levels

Every weapon and attachment has a quality, shown in game as a coloured badge with crowns. Quality sets the damage multiplier and the augment slots; each quality is a separate item with its own item ID. The colours are the ones this wiki uses for each quality.

From the game files
The Kvm 89 is the one weapon whose experimental version (×1) has a lower multiplier than its Special copy (×1.1). The Kotenok and S21 fire ordinary 7.62mm (33 per bullet) but their high multipliers make it 46.2 and 52.8 per shot at Special.

Experimental weapons15 weapons

Experimental weapons are a sixth quality (pink badge) of some base-game weapons, each with a special behaviour – a railgun Pvg 90, flechette shotgun shells, an electric sledgehammer. In the files an experimental weapon is the weapon's Q6 item with its own ammo values (experimental_ammo_overrides). Under the Gen0 Editor's slot rules they take no augments, and the experimental-only ammo types (corrosive, shock, tar, HEAT, medical, radioactive...) are made for them.
