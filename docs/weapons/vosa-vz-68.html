The Vosa vz.68 is a submachine gun that fires .32 ACP ammo, from the Eastern European Weapons Pack. It comes in 5 qualities, Dilapidated to Special. There is no experimental version. With .32 ACP Full Metal Jacket Ammo one shot does 25 damage at its best quality.
