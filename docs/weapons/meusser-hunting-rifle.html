The Meusser Hunting Rifle is a hunting rifle that fires .243 ammo, in the base game. It comes in 5 qualities, Dilapidated to Special, plus an experimental version, the Experimental Meusser Hunting Rifle. With .243 Full Metal Jacket Ammo one shot does 100 damage at its best quality.
