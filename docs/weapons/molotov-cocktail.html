Molotov Cocktail

The Molotov Cocktail is a throwable from the Tactical Equipment Pack. Its blast does 25 damage to machines within 1.5 m.

Damage

A throwable has no quality: every grenade is the same. Its damage is a blast where it lands (and for the Molotov, fire that keeps burning).

Standing in the fire for its full 7 seconds adds up to 70 damage per target on top of the blast.

It can be crafted with the Molotov Cocktail schematic (4 Accelerant, 2 Lead).
