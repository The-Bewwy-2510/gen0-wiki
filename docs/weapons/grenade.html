Grenade

The Grenade is a throwable in the base game. Its blast does 440 damage to machines within 8 m.

Damage

A throwable has no quality: every grenade is the same. Its damage is a blast where it lands (and for the Molotov, fire that keeps burning).

It can be crafted with the Grenade schematic (2 Explosive, 6 Phosphorus).
