The N9 Handgun is a handgun that fires 9mm ammo, from the US Weapons Pack. It comes in 5 qualities, Dilapidated to Special. There is no experimental version. With 9mm FMJ Ammo (Handgun) one shot does 28 damage at its best quality.

Where to find it

Ammo and related weapons

Ammo it loads: 9mm Armor-Piercing Ammo (Handgun), 9mm FMJ Ammo (Handgun).

Experimental-only rounds of this calibre (not usable by this weapon, which has no experimental version): 9mm Handgun HEAT Ammo, 9mm Handgun Shock Ammo.

Other weapons that load the same ammo: Klaucke 17, Volkov Pistol.

Same calibre family (9mm ammo), different ammo items: HP5, M/46 "Kpist" SMG, COM-10 SMG.

Other handguns: .44 Magnus, Möller PP, Klaucke 17, Volkov Pistol, Iron Condor.
