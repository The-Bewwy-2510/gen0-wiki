Overview

Attachments come in four groups by where they go on the gun: a sight, a vision module, a muzzle attachment and a magazine. Silencers, compensators, barrel extensions and chokes all go on the muzzle, so a weapon carries one of them at a time. Attachments come in five qualities like weapons (Dilapidated to Special), except vision modules, which have one. Every attachment fits only the weapons in its compatibility list; the tables below show them all.

The game files used for this wiki list which weapon each attachment fits, but not its exact effect (zoom steps, recoil or noise reduction, extra rounds per magazine, the difference between qualities). The effects below are described in words for that reason.

Scopes and sights9 items

Scopes replace the iron sights and magnify the view by the power in their name: a 4-8x scope zooms between 4 and 8 times. Higher zoom helps at long range but narrows your view, so match the scope to the range you fight at. The bow sight and the RLG-7 scope are made for those weapons only.

Vision modules7 items

Vision modules add vision modes to the weapon's sight. Low light amplifies what little light there is (night vision), IR shows heat, so machines stand out against the cold background, and the object penetrating mode shows machines through cover. The dual and full modules combine two or all three modes in one item. They come in a single quality only.

Compensators3 items

A compensator sits on the muzzle and redirects the gas from each shot to reduce recoil and muzzle climb, so long bursts stay on target. It takes the muzzle slot, so you can't use it together with a silencer.

Silencers7 items

A silencer makes your shots much quieter, so machines further away don't hear you fire. That keeps a fight small and lets you pick off machines one at a time. It takes the muzzle slot.
