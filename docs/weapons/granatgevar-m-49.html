The Granatgevär m/49 is a launcher that fires Smoke Round, High-Explosive Dual-Purpose Round, Electromagnetic Pulse Round, in the base game. It comes in 5 qualities, Dilapidated to Special, plus an experimental version, the Experimental Grg m/49.

Experimental versionExperimental Grg m/49

Experimental Grg m/49 is the Experimental version of this weapon. Its damage multiplier is ×1 (Special: ×1). Item ID 2237628567. See experimental weapons for how they work.

The files hold no separate ammo values for the experimental version: it fires the normal ammo above, and its special behaviour lives in files the extractor doesn't read.
