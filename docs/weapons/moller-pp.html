Ammo and related weapons

Ammo it loads: .32 ACP Full Metal Jacket Ammo, .32 ACP Hollow-Point Ammo.

Experimental-only rounds of this calibre (not usable by this weapon, which has no experimental version): .32 ACP Radioactive Ammo, .32 ACP Medical Ammo.

Other weapons that load the same ammo: Vosa vz.68.

Other handguns: .44 Magnus, Klaucke 17, Volkov Pistol, N9 Handgun, Iron Condor.
