HP5

The HP5 is a submachine gun that fires 9mm ammo, in the base game. It comes in 5 qualities, Dilapidated to Special, plus an experimental version, the Experimental HP5 SMG.

Experimental versionExperimental HP5 SMG

Experimental HP5 SMG is the Experimental version of this weapon. Its damage multiplier is ×1 (Special: ×1). See experimental weapons for the other experimental weapons.

The files hold no separate ammo values for the experimental version: it fires the normal ammo above, and its special behaviour lives in files the extractor doesn't read.

Ammo and related weapons

Ammo it loads: 9mm FMJ Ammo (SMG), 9mm Armor-Piercing Ammo (SMG).

Experimental-only rounds (for the experimental version): 9mm SMG Explosive Ammo, 9mm SMG Corrosive Ammo.

Other weapons that load the same ammo: M/46 "Kpist" SMG, COM-10 SMG.

Same calibre family (9mm ammo), different ammo items: Klaucke 17, Volkov Pistol, N9 Handgun.

Other submachine guns: M/46 "Kpist" SMG, Vosa vz.68, COM-10 SMG.
