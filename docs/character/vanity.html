Emotes, vehicles & more

Besides apparel, the game's vanity list has face paint, emotes (gestures, dances and companion tricks), vehicle paint jobs for the bikes, weapon skins and companion cosmetics: 1,483 vanity items in all. This page lists face paint, emotes and vehicles in full; weapon skins and companion parts have their own pages. Like apparel, unlocked vanity items are shared by every character on a profile. Pack names other than Base game, Alpine Unrest and FNIX Rising are inferred from their contents (see Item packs).

Emotes59 items

Emotes are the gestures and dances your character can perform; you pick which ones go in your emote slots. There are 59 in the files: 26 gestures, 30 dances, 3 companion tricks. 22 base game emotes start unlocked.

Dances from videotapes
The 9 base game dances that start locked are Bossy, J-Schwiss, Mill Swing, Royalty, Serpentine, Shoulder Swing, Spacewalk, The Business, Twisting. All 9 share their name with one of the Videotape collectibles; picking up the tape is how you get the dance.
