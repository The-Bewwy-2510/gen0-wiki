Emotes & face paint

Emotes are the gestures and dances your character can perform; you choose which ones sit in your emote wheel. Face paint goes over your character's face in the looks menu. The game has 59 emotes and 34 face paints. Like apparel, they belong to your profile, so every character on it can use them.

How you get it
  • Unlocked Every profile has it from the start.
  • With its pack Unlocked as soon as you own its pack (or add it, for the free packs).
  • Find or earn Not given automatically: found or earned in the game.
  • Find or earn (pack) Needs its pack, and then you still find or earn it in the game.

Read from each item's lock flag in the game files. No file says what the flag means; this is how the Gen0 Editor reads it.
