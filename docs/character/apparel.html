Apparel

Apparel is the clothing your character wears: 1,209 items in 12 slots, from hats and hair to shoes and backpacks. Many items are colour versions of the same piece, so they are grouped here into 273 cards. Unlocked apparel is kept in the profile's item stash (Profile.ItemStash), so it is shared by every character on the profile. The items on this page change your looks; bonuses such as bullet resistance come from the apparel crafting schematics.

How to read the data

Every apparel item in the game files has a display name, an internal name (char_part_…), a slot, an icon and the hash of the content pack it belongs to. Three more fields matter for players:

  • Lock flag: the item's starting state. U means unlocked from the start, L not given automatically (found, earned, or chosen in the character creator); a trailing D marks downloadable content. No game file says what the letters mean: this reading is inferred from how the Gen0 Editor uses them (it marks UD and LD items as DLC). It doesn't fit every slot: the heads and hands (slot body) are all L, yet you choose them at character creation.
  • Model: colour versions of the same piece of clothing share one model hash. The cards below group by name instead, because a few model hashes are shared by differently named pieces (for example Band tee and Lousy T-shirt).
  • Body: a handful of items (one hairstyle and the heads and hands) are made for one body only, marked Female or Male. Everything else fits both.

From the game files
A content pack is how the game files group content; it is not always something you buy. Many packs hold items you get in the game for free (rewards, events), and the lock flag tells you which. See Item packs for what each pack contains.
