Apparel

Apparel is the clothing your character wears: 1,209 items in 12 slots, from hats and hair to shoes and backpacks. Most pieces come in several colours, so they make 273 different pieces. Unlocked apparel belongs to your profile, so every character on it can wear it. Apparel only changes your looks; the bonuses (such as bullet resistance) come from the apparel crafting schematics.

How you get it
  • Unlocked Every profile has it from the start.
  • With its pack Unlocked as soon as you own its pack (or add it, for the free packs).

Read from each item's lock flag in the game files. No file says what the flag means; this is how the Gen0 Editor reads it.

Good to know

  • The pictures show the slot, not the look. The game files give most apparel one generic icon per slot, so only a few pieces (the Experimental set, for example) have a picture of their own.
  • Heads and hands are picked in the character creator, whatever their lock flag says. A few of them, and one hairstyle, are made for one body only (marked female or male).
  • Packs are how the game files group content; many packs are free (rewards, events, updates). Every pack tag links to its entry on the DLC page.
