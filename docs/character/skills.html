Skills

Skills are the perks you buy with skill points as your character levels up. There are 48 skills in four trees: Combat, Support, Survival, Tech. Each tree has two columns of six skills, and the last skill of every column is a specialization, an ability you switch on. Every rank costs one point; maxing everything takes 87 points.

Skill points and tips

Each level-up gives you a skill point, and a rank of a skill costs one point. One rank is enough to open the next skill in the column, so the cheapest way down a column is one point per skill: any specialization costs at least 6 points (one rank in each of the five skills above it, plus the specialization itself).

  • One-point skills give their full effect at once: Aim Speed, Healer, Steady Feet, Down But Not Out, Chemist, Lockpicking. Of these, Lockpicking (open locked doors with lockpicks) and Healer (heal other players with a first aid kit) add abilities rather than bonuses.
  • Armor protects against a different damage type per level: level 1 is bullets, level 2 explosives, level 3 gas. Chemist adds another 10% gas resistance for one point.
  • Each damage skill covers one kind of hit: Armor Damage (Combat) on armor, Component Damage (Tech) on components, Trigger Happy on automatic fire, and Make 'Em Count on components with single shots from automatic weapons.
  • Binocular skills (Enemy Marking, Designated Target, Spotting Intel, Remote Hacking) all work through the binoculars, so keep them on you.
  • Inquisitive Mind doubles mission XP at level 2 (+100%), so it pays back fastest when you take it early.
