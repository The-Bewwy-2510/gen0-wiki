Skills

Your character has 48 skills in four trees (Combat, Support, Survival, Tech). Each tree has two columns of six skills; a skill opens once the skill above it is learned, and the bottom skill of every column is a specialization, an ability you switch on. Ranks cost one skill point each, and maxing every skill takes 87 skill points. All names, descriptions and level values below are the game's own text.

Skill points

Each level-up gives you a skill point. A rank of a skill costs one point, so a skill with three levels costs three points to max. One rank in a skill is enough to open the next skill in its column (the Gen0 Editor's skill tab works the same way), which means the cheapest way down a column is one point per skill.

The table shows each column of each tree: its first skill and the specialization at the bottom, and how many points it takes to max the whole column. Reaching any specialization takes 6 points (one rank in each of the five skills above it, plus the specialization itself).

Tips from the numbers

  • Every specialization costs at least 6 points: five skills above it at one rank each, plus the specialization's single rank. With 8 specializations and only one active at a time, it's worth getting one early and filling in the rest later.
  • One-point skills give their full effect at once: Aim Speed, Healer, Steady Feet, Down But Not Out, Chemist, Lockpicking. Of these, Lockpicking (open locked doors with lockpicks) and Healer (heal other players with a first aid kit) add abilities rather than bonuses.
  • Armor protects against a different damage type per level: level 1 is bullets, level 2 explosives, level 3 gas. If gas is your problem you need all three ranks; Chemist adds another 10% gas resistance for one point.
  • Each damage skill covers one kind of hit: Armor Damage (Combat) works on armor and Component Damage (Tech) on components, Trigger Happy on automatic fire, and Make 'Em Count adds component damage for single shots from automatic weapons, so it works alongside Component Damage rather than as a general damage boost.
  • Binocular skills (Enemy Marking, Designated Target, Spotting Intel, Remote Hacking) all work through the binoculars, so keep them on you.
  • Inquisitive Mind doubles mission XP at level 2 (+100%), so it pays back fastest when you take it early.
